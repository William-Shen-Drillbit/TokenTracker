How these estimates work

Allowances are modeling assumptions, not provider entitlements. Historical usage is estimated against the sole observed account where available; multiple possible accounts remain unallocated. Allowances cover the full selected period, including days with no recorded usage. Person-level priced usage excludes unpriced tokens; account estimates may include a labeled price proxy.
